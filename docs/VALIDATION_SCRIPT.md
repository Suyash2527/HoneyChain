# 10-minute validation interviews (do these for real, then quote them honestly)

Goal: talk to **5 beekeepers, 2 lab people, 1 KVIC / NBB field officer, 1-2 honey buyers** before or right after the SIH submission.
Judges respect a small number of *real* conversations far more than an impressive-sounding invented one.

## How to find people fast (today)
- KVIC / State Khadi & Village Industries Board district office - ask for the honey / beekeeping extension officer.
- Local beekeepers' cooperatives or FPOs; sellers of raw honey at farmers' markets; agriculture college (KVK) staff.
- NABL-accredited food-testing labs (ask for the food-testing head, 5 minutes).
- Call, WhatsApp, or visit. Say: "College hackathon project, 10 minutes, no selling."

## Script (beekeeper)
1. How many hives do you run, and where do you sell? At what price per kg?
2. Have buyers ever doubted your honey is pure? What happened?
3. What do you lose most: colonies (disease, swarming) or price?
4. Show the phone demo (Verify Honey -> HC-2026-0001, then the fake one). *"Would a QR like this help you sell? What would you change?"*
5. Would you enter a harvest on a phone? In which language? Who would help you?
6. What internet do you have at the apiary?

## Script (lab / KVIC officer)
1. Which tests do you run for honey today (moisture, HMF, C4, NMR, SMR)? Turnaround and cost per sample?
2. Who could publish a result to a shared ledger, and what approval would that need?
3. Where would this break in real life (data entry, fraud, cost)?
4. Show the Lab Tests screen. *"Is this the right set of fields?"*

## Script (buyer)
1. How do you decide which honey to trust today?
2. Would you scan a QR in a shop? What would you want to see in 5 seconds?
